Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

AccessoRex is rated 4+ and designed to be safe for all ages. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no backend of its own. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are shown through Google AdMob, configured for a family audience, so they are not personalized and are age-appropriate. The app does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not ask for tracking permission. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Who we are

AccessoRex is published by Perception Labs LLC ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support@accessorex.app.

Age rating

AccessoRex is rated 4+ and intended to be suitable for all ages.

No accounts, no server

The app has no sign-in and no backend of its own. Your game progress — level, high score, coins, unlocks and the current outfit — is stored only on your device and is never sent to Perception Labs. Deleting the app erases this data.

Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob is configured for a family audience, so ads shown in AccessoRex are not personalized and are age-appropriate.

The app does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not ask for tracking permission (no App Tracking Transparency prompt).

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may still process limited technical information, such as IP address, device type and ad interactions. You can read how Google handles data for its advertising partners here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Any in-app purchases (such as Remove Ads) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Perception Labs never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Contact

Perception Labs only receives personal information if you choose to email us at support@accessorex.app. We use your message only to reply to you. We don't share it and we don't add you to any mailing list.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown in AccessoRex are non-personalized. If a parent or guardian has a concern, please email support@accessorex.app and we'll respond quickly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the updated version on this page and update the "Last updated" date at the top.
